Pigment Perfecting Serum

$64 30 mL

Alpha-arbutin and Bidens pilosa extract fade dark spots and even skin tone, on a lightweight hyaluronic-acid base.

A lightweight serum built around alpha-arbutin, a brightening active, with Bidens pilosa extract and Ascophyllum nodosum (brown algae) extract behind it. Hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid and sodium hyaluronate carry the hydration. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, one hundred percent natural origin, twenty-three percent organic origin.

Key ingredients
  • Alpha-Arbutin
  • Bidens Pilosa Extract
  • Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid / Sodium Hyaluronate

Bidens pilosa (Spanish needle)
Literal in the INCI as Bidens Pilosa Extract. Alpha-arbutin is the formula's headline brightening active but is a synthesised molecule, not a plant part, so Bidens pilosa — the strongest named botanical — is the signature drawn here.
Vessel
30 mL frosted glass bottle with a dropper.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle. The dropper's bulb and collar are mixed materials and are usually not accepted kerbside — separate them and check your local stream.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
