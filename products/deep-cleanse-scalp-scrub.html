Deep Cleanse Scalp Scrub, Rosemary & Mint

$38 170 mL

Sour cherry shell and kaolin lift buildup from the scalp; rosemary and peppermint leave a cool, clean prickle.

Sour cherry shell powder and kaolin clay work as gentle exfoliants against scalp buildup, while rosemary and peppermint oil bring the cool prickle that signals the scalp is actually being worked. An ECOCERT COSMOS NATURAL certified formula, ninety-nine percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Rosemary Leaf Oil
  • Peppermint Oil

How to use
