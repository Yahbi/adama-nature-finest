Sensitive Scalp Shampoo

$34 400 mL

A gentle shampoo for dry and sensitive scalps, with plum, linden and jasmine — the hair companion to the sensitive skin line.

Expertly formulated to care for dry and sensitive skin, this gentle yet effective shampoo suits all hair types and is dermatologically tested for delicate or easily irritated scalps. Plum fruit extract, linden flower extract and jasmine extract sit behind a coco-sulfate base — the same trio as Adama's Sensitive Skin Face & Body Cleanser, of which this shampoo is the hair companion. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-eight percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein
  • Prunus Domestica (Plum) Fruit Extract
  • Tilia Cordata (Linden) Flower Extract
  • Jasminum Officinale (Jasmine) Extract
How to use
Apply to wet hair and scalp, lather, and rinse thoroughly.

① Organic farming

Linden (Tilia cordata)
Literal in the INCI as Tilia Cordata (Linden) Flower Extract, organic-farmed, alongside jasmine and plum — the same signature trio as the live Sensitive Skin Face & Body Cleanser. Selfnamed markets this shampoo as the hair companion in that sensitive-skincare line, reusing the same Linden plate.
Allergen note
Contains Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein — a wheat/gluten derivative — worth flagging for a gluten sensitivity or wheat allergy. Correctly not badged Gluten Free by the source.
Vessel
400 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
