Anti-Age Night Cream

$64 50 mL

A marine ferment complex works overnight for firmer skin; cocoa and shea butter hydrate deeply.

A night cream built around an Alteromonas ferment extract — described by the maker as a marine plankton extract — that works overnight, paired with cocoa seed butter and shea butter for deep hydration. Elder fruit extract and algae extract sit behind it. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin, twenty-six percent organic.
