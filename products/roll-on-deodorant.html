Roll-On Deodorant, Aluminum-Free

$26 50 mL

Lavender and rosemary over aloe and panthenol; shikimic acid works at the source of odour.

An aluminum-free formula that works by addressing odour at its source rather than blocking perspiration: shikimic acid and triethyl citrate as the deodorizing actives, with aloe vera and panthenol to keep the underarm comfortable. Lavender, rosemary and eucalyptus carry the scent.

Key ingredients
