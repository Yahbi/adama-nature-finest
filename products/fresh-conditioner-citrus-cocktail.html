Fresh Conditioner, Citrus Cocktail

$32 370 mL

A citrus-cocktail conditioner for the ends, with panthenol to ease styling and reduce breakage and split ends.

Nourishes hair ends, preventing breakage and split ends, so hair becomes silkier and easier to style. Panthenol and lemon and bitter-orange peel oils carry a fresh, sour citrus-cocktail aroma with herbal notes and a delicate touch of sweet floral petals.

Key ingredients
  • Panthenol
  • Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein
  • Citrus Limon Peel Oil
  • Citrus Aurantium Peel Oil
How to use
Apply to the ends of the hair, leave for 1–3 minutes to absorb, and rinse out.
Presentation
370 mL
How to use
Apply to the ends of the hair, leave for 1–3 minutes to absorb, and rinse out. For external use.

Full ingredient list, as declared by the manufacturer

Aqua, Cetearyl Alcohol, Betaine, Glycerin, Distearoylethyl Dimonium Chloride, Parfum, Mica, Lactic Acid, Panthenol, Benzyl Alcohol, CI 77891, Sodium Benzoate, Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein, Potassium Sorbate, Limonene, Citrus Limon Peel Oil, Citrus Aurantium Peel Oil, Citral, Pinene, Linalool, Citronellol, Terpineol, Linalyl Acetate.

Bitter orange (Citrus aurantium)
Citrus Aurantium Peel Oil is literal in the INCI, alongside Citrus Limon Peel Oil — the citrus-cocktail pairing behind the name. Reuses the existing Neroli Hydrosol plate.
Allergen note
Contains Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein — a wheat/gluten derivative — worth flagging for a gluten sensitivity or wheat allergy. No organic-farming or essential-oil markers were printed on this formula's own panel; reproduced as captured.
Vessel
370 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
