Brightening Eye Cream

$50 15 mL

Sunflower and shea carry horse chestnut and ginkgo; hyaluronic acid meets the eye area.

A sunflower-and-shea eye cream built to reduce puffiness and dark circles, with Aesculus hippocastanum (horse chestnut) seed extract and ginkgo biloba leaf extract behind it, and multi-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid for the eye area's thinnest skin. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, one hundred percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter
  • Aesculus Hippocastanum (Horse Chestnut) Seed Extract
  • Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract
  • Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid
How to use
Apply to clean skin around the eye area, morning and/or evening.
