Moisturising Shampoo

$30 290 mL

A gentle, moisturising shampoo with sea kale, ginkgo and burdock root extracts that smooths hair and adds gloss.

Gently purifies the hair, leaving it fresh and moisturised. This shampoo helps keep hair smooth and glossy, built on the same coco-sulfate surfactant base as Adama's Gloss Shampoo, with an entirely different botanical and fragrance profile — sea kale, ginkgo and burdock root extracts, over a juicy fruits and warm woods aroma. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Crambe Maritima (Sea Kale) Leaf Extract
  • Ginkgo Biloba (Ginkgo) Leaf Extract
  • Arctium Lappa (Burdock) Root Extract
How to use
Apply through damp hair and over the scalp, lather and rinse out.
