Light Moisturising Day Cream

$54 50 mL

A lightweight cream with hyaluronic acid and betaine; parsley and oak bark extract behind it.

A lightweight, fast-absorbing day cream built on argan kernel oil, with hyaluronic acid and betaine for moisture and a matte finish. Parsley extract, oak bark extract and burdock root extract sit behind it. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin, fifteen percent organic.
