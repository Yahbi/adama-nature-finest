Kojic Acid Face & Body Exfoliator

$54 150 mL

Kojic acid and fine perlite polish away dead skin, face and body, with shea butter and jojoba oil to keep it gentle.

A dual-use exfoliator for face and body, built around kojic acid and fine perlite to lift away dead skin and even out tone. Shea butter and jojoba seed oil keep the polish from stripping, with a touch of vitamin C behind it. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-eight percent natural origin, six percent organic origin.

Key ingredients
  • Kojic Acid
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter
  • Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil
How to use
Massage onto damp skin in circular motions, face or body, two to three times weekly. Rinse well, then moisturise. Kojic acid increases sun sensitivity, so follow with sunscreen by day.

How to use

Shea (Vitellaria paradoxa)
Literal in the INCI as Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, organic-farmed. Kojic acid itself is fungal-fermentation-derived, not a plant extract, so shea — the formula's next-highest botanical — is the signature drawn here.
Vessel
150 mL frosted glass jar with a bronze screw lid.
Recyclability
Scrape the jar out before disposal. Body and lid are separate parts and are best separated.

Storage. Keep the lid closed. Take the product out with clean, dry hands.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
