Dark Spot Face Cream with Kojic Acid

$59 50 mL

Kojic acid targets dark spots and discoloration, with sea buckthorn and rosemary extract for antioxidant support.

A targeted face cream for dark spots and discoloration, built around kojic acid with sea buckthorn fruit oil and rosemary leaf extract behind it for antioxidant support and hydration. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, one hundred percent natural origin, eight percent organic origin.

Key ingredients
  • Kojic Acid
  • Hippophae Rhamnoides (Sea Buckthorn) Fruit Oil
  • Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Extract
How to use
Apply once or twice daily to clean, dry skin, focusing on areas of discoloration. Kojic acid increases sun sensitivity, so always wear sunscreen by day.
