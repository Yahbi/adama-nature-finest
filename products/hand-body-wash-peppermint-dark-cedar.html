Hand & Body Wash, Peppermint & Dark Cedar

Hydrating betaine and quince fruit extract behind a gentle cleanse, in a fresh mint-and-cedarwood aroma.

A gentle hand and body wash built on sodium coco-sulfate and betaine, with quince fruit extract behind them, in a fresh aroma of mint and dark cedarwood.
