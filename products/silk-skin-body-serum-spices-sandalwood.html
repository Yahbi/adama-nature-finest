Silk Skin Body Serum, Spices & Sandalwood

The same fast-absorbing jojoba-and-rosehip base as its sibling, in a citrus, cardamom and sandalwood aroma.

The same fast-absorbing body serum base as the Woody Amber & Leather variant — jojoba seed oil and rosehip fruit oil, with sodium hyaluronate and ginkgo biloba leaf extract behind them — in a brighter fragrance: citrus and spicy pink pepper, cardamom and ginger, over a woody sandalwood-and-vetiver background.
