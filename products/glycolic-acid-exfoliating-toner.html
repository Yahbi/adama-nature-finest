Glycolic Acid Exfoliating Toner

$46 250 mL

Five percent glycolic acid with cranberry and lingonberry extract behind it, evening out tone.

A five percent glycolic acid toner meant to lift surface buildup and even out tone with regular use, softened by cranberry and lingonberry fruit extract. Suits an evening routine on clean, dry skin, followed the next day by sunscreen.

Key ingredients
