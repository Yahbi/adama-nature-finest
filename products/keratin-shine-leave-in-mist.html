Keratin Shine Leave-In Hair Mist

A lightweight leave-in mist with hydrolyzed keratin and red algae extract to smooth, strengthen and detangle damaged hair.

A lightweight leave-in mist infused with hydrolyzed keratin, lactic acid and algae extract, working to smooth, strengthen and detangle damaged hair. It helps reduce breakage and split ends, leaving hair healthier, softer and more manageable. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin.

① From natural essential oils

Bitter orange (Citrus aurantium)
Citrus Aurantium Peel Oil is literal in the INCI. Furcellaria Lumbricalis is a red algae, not a land plant, so bitter orange — the strongest named botanical — is the signature drawn here, reusing the existing Neroli Hydrosol plate.
Vessel
100 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
