Volcanic Sand Body Polish

$40 170 mL

Volcanic sand milled fine as flour, carried in castor oil.

Icelandic volcanic sand milled fine as flour, held in castor oil and kaolin, with blueberry, strawberry, cloudberry and raspberry. Abrasive enough to work, fine enough not to scratch.

Key ingredients
  • Volcanic Sand
  • Kaolin
  • Hydrogenated Castor Oil
How to use
On damp skin, massage in circular motions, then rinse. Once or twice weekly.

Recyclability
Scrape the jar out before disposal. Body and lid are separate parts and are best separated; check that your local stream accepts each.

Storage. Keep the lid closed. Take the polish out with dry hands — water in the jar shortens its life.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
