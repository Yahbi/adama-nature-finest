Scalp Care Exfoliating Serum

$36 30 mL

A fragrance-free scalp serum with lactic acid to gently exfoliate, and sodium hyaluronate and nettle leaf extract to keep the scalp comfortable.

Fragrance-free

An exfoliating scalp serum designed to gently remove product buildup while purifying and rebalancing the scalp. Lactic acid softly exfoliates and renews the scalp surface, while sodium hyaluronate delivers lightweight hydration; nettle leaf extract behind it relieves discomfort and dryness. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, one hundred percent natural origin. No fragrance of any kind, declared or otherwise.

Key ingredients
  • Lactic Acid
  • Sodium Hyaluronate
  • Urtica Dioica (Nettle) Leaf Extract
How to use
Apply to a dry scalp, leave on for 3–5 minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Once or twice weekly.

Recyclability
Rinse the bottle. The dropper's bulb and collar are mixed materials and are usually not accepted kerbside — separate them and check your local stream.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
