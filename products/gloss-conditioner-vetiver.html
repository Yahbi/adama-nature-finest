Gloss Conditioner, Smoky Green Vetiver

$32 370 mL

Avocado oil and panthenol close the cuticle; cedarwood and patchouli carry the smoke-green note.

Avocado oil and panthenol sit over the cuticle to close it, with behentrimonium chloride and hydrolyzed wheat protein doing the conditioning work underneath. The same cedarwood-and-patchouli fragrance family as the Gloss Shampoo, meant to be used together.

Key ingredients
  • Avocado Oil
  • Panthenol
  • Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein
How to use
Apply to the ends of the hair, leave for 1–3 minutes to absorb, and rinse out.
Presentation
370 mL
How to use
Apply to the ends of the hair, leave for 1–3 minutes to absorb, and rinse out. For external use.

Full ingredient list, as declared by the manufacturer

Aqua, Cetearyl Alcohol, Behentrimonium Chloride, Betaine, Glycerin, Panthenol, Distearoylethyl Dimonium Chloride, Persea Gratissima (Avocado) Oil†, Parfum, Mica, Lactic Acid, Tocopherol, Benzyl Alcohol, CI 77891, Sodium Benzoate, Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein, Potassium Sorbate, Limonene, Citrus Limon Peel Oil, Pogostemon Cablin Oil, Juniperus Virginiana Oil, Linalool.

† Ingredients from organic farming

Cedarwood (Juniperus virginiana)
Same fragrance family as the Gloss Shampoo; cedarwood and patchouli carry the smoky-green note rather than a literal vetiver plant.
Vessel
370 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
