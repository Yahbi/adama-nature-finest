BiPhasic Make-up Remover

$34 100 mL

A fragrance-free two-phase remover; aloe, sea-buckthorn and camomile lift make-up clean.

Fragrance-free

An oil-and-water two-phase make-up remover, shaken together before use. Aloe leaf juice carries the water phase; sea-buckthorn and camomile extracts sit behind it, with sodium hyaluronate for hydration. No fragrance of any kind. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice
  • Sodium Hyaluronate
  • Hippophae Rhamnoides (Sea-Buckthorn) Fruit Extract
  • Chamomilla Recutita (Camomile) Flower Extract
How to use
Shake well. Sweep over closed eyes and face with a cotton pad; no rinsing required.
