Ferment & Niacinamide Serum

$66 30 mL

Niacinamide and a microbiome-supporting ferment complex strengthen the barrier; centella soothes.

Fragrance-free

An advanced serum that supports skin renewal and helps improve the appearance of dullness and uneven tone. Niacinamide and a microbiome-supporting ferment complex — Lactobacillus Ferment and phospholipids — work to strengthen the skin barrier, while lactic acid gently refines the complexion and hyaluronic acid delivers lightweight hydration. Centella asiatica leaf extract soothes. Fragrance-free; absorbs quickly and layers into AM/PM routines.
