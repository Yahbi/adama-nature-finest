Urea & Panthenol Body Cream

$44 280 mL

Sunflower oil bodied with panthenol and hyaluronic acid; 5% urea for rough, dry skin that needs more than a lotion.

Sunflower seed oil forms the base, with panthenol and hyaluronic acid for moisture retention and five percent urea to work on rough, dry patches directly — elbows, heels, hands. A heavier cream than the house's existing Comforting Body Cream, built for skin that a lotion has stopped helping.

Key ingredients
  • Sunflower Seed Oil
  • Urea (5%)
  • Panthenol
How to use
Massage into clean skin, morning or after bathing. For elbows, heels and hands especially.
Presentation
280 mL
How to use

Sunflower (Helianthus annuus)
The lead emollient oil, literal in the INCI; the cucumber and thyme notes in the fragrance are scent character, not named actives.
Vessel
280 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
