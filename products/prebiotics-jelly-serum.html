Bioactive Prebiotics Jelly Serum

$52 30 mL

A lightweight jelly that supports the skin's protective barrier and leaves a soothed, radiant finish.

A featherlight jelly serum led by the same prebiotic complex as the Microbiome Face Mist — Lactobacillus ferment lysate — with algin and hyaluronic acid layered underneath to support the skin’s barrier and hold moisture through the day. Patchouli oil is the only scent note; there is no botanical extract to draw here.

Key ingredients
  • Lactobacillus Ferment Lysate
  • Algin
  • Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
