Keratin Volume Boost Shampoo

$34 390 mL

A volumizing shampoo with hydrolyzed keratin and caffeine that purifies and reactivates the scalp for fuller-looking hair.

A gentle yet powerful formula that purifies strands, leaving hair noticeably soft, smooth and volumized. Hydrolyzed keratin and a hydrating complex promote a fuller appearance while helping protect against breakage; caffeine and rosemary leaf oil sit behind it, on the same gourmand peppermint aroma as the matching conditioner. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin, four percent organic origin.

Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)
Literal in the INCI as Rosmarinus Officinalis Leaf Oil, organic-farmed. Cantharellus Cibarius (Chanterelle) Extract is a fungal extract, not a plant, so rosemary — shared with the matching conditioner — is the signature botanical drawn here.

Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
