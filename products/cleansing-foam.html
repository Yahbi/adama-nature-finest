Cleansing Foam

$36 150 mL

Rose water foams soft around calendula and camomile, lifting make-up and the day together.

A mild, minimalist foaming cleanser built on Damask rose flower water, with calendula and camomile extracts to calm the skin as it cleanses. Sucrose cocoate keeps the lather light rather than stripping. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-seven percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Rosa Damascena (Rose) Flower Water
  • Calendula Officinalis (Calendula) Flower Extract
  • Chamomilla Recutita (Camomile) Flower Extract
How to use
Massage into damp skin, working to a light foam, then rinse with water.
Presentation
150 mL
How to use

Recyclability

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
