Keratin Volume Boost Conditioner

$34 390 mL

A volumizing conditioner with hydrolyzed keratin and caffeine, smoothing and detangling for visibly fuller hair.

A volumizing conditioner that smooths, detangles and protects against breakage and frizz, enriched with nourishing botanical extracts and hydrolyzed keratin to fortify strands from within. Caffeine and rosemary leaf oil sit behind it, on a gourmand aroma of peppermint and vanilla-sweet tonka bean. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-eight percent natural origin, seven percent organic origin.

Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)
Literal in the INCI as Rosmarinus Officinalis Leaf Oil, organic-farmed, alongside peppermint oil in the aroma. Cantharellus Cibarius (Chanterelle) Extract is a fungal extract, not a plant, so rosemary is the signature botanical drawn here — the same plate as the Rosemary Scalp Oil.
Allergen note
Contains Persea Gratissima (Avocado) Oil and Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, both organic-farmed — worth flagging for a nut or seed-oil sensitivity.
Vessel
390 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
