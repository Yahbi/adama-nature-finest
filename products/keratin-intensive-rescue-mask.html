Keratin Intensive Rescue Hair Mask

$40 170 mL

An intensive hair mask with hydrolyzed keratin, wheat proteins and shea butter to reinforce structure and add shine.

An intensive hair mask that deeply nourishes and strengthens hair, targeting breakage, split ends and dullness. Hydrolyzed keratin, wheat proteins, peptides and a biomineral complex reinforce hair structure and improve elasticity and shine, while vitamin E and shea butter provide antioxidant protection and intense hydration. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-eight percent natural origin, twelve percent organic origin.

* Organic

Shea (Vitellaria paradoxa)
Literal in the INCI as Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, organic-farmed. Cantharellus Cibarius (Chanterelle) Extract is a fungal extract, not a plant, so shea — the formula's next-highest botanical — is the signature drawn here.
Allergen note
Contains Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein (gluten) and two tree-nut-adjacent oils — Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil and Persea Gratissima (Avocado) Oil, both organic-farmed — worth flagging for a gluten, nut or seed-oil sensitivity. Not badged Gluten Free by the source.
Vessel
170 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
