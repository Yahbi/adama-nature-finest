Vitamin C Serum

$58 30 mL

Two percent natural vitamin C and one percent ferulic acid brighten and firm, with sea buckthorn oil behind them.

A brightening serum built on two percent Ascorbyl Glucoside — a stable, natural form of vitamin C — with one percent ferulic acid for firmness and sea buckthorn fruit oil behind it. Hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid and sodium hyaluronate carry the hydration. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin, thirteen percent organic origin.

Recyclability
Rinse the bottle. The dropper's bulb and collar are mixed materials and are usually not accepted kerbside — separate them and check your local stream.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
