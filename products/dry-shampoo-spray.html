Quick Refresh Dry Shampoo Spray

$32 100 mL

A fine mist that absorbs excess sebum at the roots and refreshes hair instantly between washes.

A non-aerosol dry shampoo in a fine, lightweight mist. It absorbs excess sebum at the roots and refreshes hair instantly between washes, with echinacea and rhodiola extract behind it. Suitable for all hair types.

Key ingredients
  • Echinacea Purpurea Extract
  • Rhodiola Rosea Extract
  • Panthenol
How to use
Shake well before use. Spray onto dry hair, focusing on the roots, then gently massage with fingertips. Do not rinse.
Presentation
100 mL
How to use
Shake well before use. Spray onto dry hair, focusing on the roots, then gently massage with fingertips. Do not rinse. For external use.

Full ingredient list, as declared by the manufacturer

Alcohol denat., Aqua (water), Distarch phosphate, Propanediol, Glycerin, Parfum (fragrance), Sarcosine, Panthenol, Maltodextrin, Echinacea Purpurea Extract, Rhodiola Rosea Extract.

Echinacea (Echinacea purpurea)
Literal in the INCI as Echinacea Purpurea Extract, with rhodiola rosea extract behind it.
Vessel
100 mL recyclable plastic pump bottle.
Recyclability
Rinse the bottle before recycling. The pump assembly is mixed materials and is usually not accepted kerbside.

Storage. Keep the vessel closed, away from direct sun and heat.

Precautions. External use only. Avoid the eye area unless the directions say otherwise. If irritation occurs, stop using it. Not a medicine, and no medical claim is made for it.
