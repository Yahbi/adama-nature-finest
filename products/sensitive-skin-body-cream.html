Sensitive Skin Body Cream

$38 190 mL

Shea and cocoa butters for daily hydration, with linden, jasmine and plum extracts, calmed for sensitive skin.

A gentle, fast-absorbing body cream built for sensitive skin, suitable for daily use on both face and body. Shea and cocoa seed butters carry the hydration, with linden flower, jasmine and plum fruit extracts behind them. It is the line-companion to the Sensitive Skin Face & Body Cleanser, sharing the same calming linden-jasmine-plum trio in a cream format. Ecocert COSMOS Organic certified, ninety-nine percent natural origin, twenty percent organic origin.

Key ingredients
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter
  • Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Seed Butter
  • Tilia Cordata (Linden) Flower Extract
  • Prunus Domestica (Plum) Fruit Extract
How to use
Apply daily to clean skin, face and body.
