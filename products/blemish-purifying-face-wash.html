Blemish Purifying Face Wash

$38 140 mL

A pH-balanced face wash for blemish-prone skin, with mate leaf, Iceland moss and juniper callus extract to purify without stripping.

A gentle, pH-balanced wash for blemish-prone skin that removes impurities and excess sebum without harsh surfactants. Mate leaf, Iceland moss and juniper callus extract behind it are soothing and purifying. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, ninety-eight percent natural origin.

Key ingredients
  • Ilex Paraguariensis (Mate) Leaf Extract
  • Cetraria Islandica (Iceland Moss) Extract
  • Juniperus Communis (Juniper) Callus Extract
  • Kaolin
How to use
Apply a small amount to damp skin, gently massage, then rinse thoroughly. Morning and evening.
