Blemish Care Moisturiser

$58 50 mL

A lightweight moisturiser for blemish-prone skin, with argan oil and hyaluronic acid, plus mate leaf, Iceland moss and juniper behind them.

A lightweight moisturiser that hydrates and soothes blemish-prone skin without clogging pores. Argan kernel oil and sodium PCA support hydration and softness; an antioxidant complex of vitamin C, mate leaf, Iceland moss and juniper callus extract protects and calms behind it. Ecocert COSMOS Natural certified, one hundred percent natural origin, nine percent organic origin.

Key ingredients
  • Argania Spinosa (Argan) Kernel Oil
  • Sodium Hyaluronate
  • Ascorbyl Palmitate (Vitamin C)
  • Ilex Paraguariensis (Mate) Leaf Extract
How to use
Apply a pea-sized amount to clean skin on the face and neck, gently massage until fully absorbed. Morning and evening.
