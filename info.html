Practical matters

The house is small and speaks plainly. What follows is what we can promise today; anything we cannot yet promise is marked as such.

Shipping & returns

Dispatch

Adama is taking advance orders. Each formulation ships when its first production run is bottled and released — orders are dispatched in the sequence they were received, and you will be written to when yours leaves.

Shipping

Shipping is complimentary on orders over $75. Below that, the exact carrier rate is calculated at checkout — we do not mark it up.

Returns

An unopened vessel may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened formulations cannot be resold and cannot be returned, except where the product is faulty — in which case we replace or refund it, whichever you prefer.

The full carrier list and international duties table will be published here when the store opens.

Questions, asked often

When will my advance order arrive?

When the first run of that formulation is finished. Cold-pressed oils and small batches move at their own pace; we will not hurry either. Your order email carries the current estimate.

Are the formulations vegan?

All nine are. Every material in the range is plant- or mineral-derived — no honey, beeswax, lanolin or carmine appears in any declaration. Nothing is tested on animals, by us or on our behalf.

Are there allergens I should know about?

Sweet almond oil is in the Nourishing Body Oil, the Rosemary Scalp Oil and both facial oils; argan is in six of the nine. If you react to tree nuts, read the full list on the product page first. Several formulations also carry fragrance components — linalool, limonene, citral and geraniol among them — declared by name. The Soothing Overnight Cream and the Restorative Face Cream carry no fragrance at all.

What is printed on the label?

The name of the formulation, what it is, its signature botanical in Latin, the net contents, the directions and the full ingredient list as the manufacturer declares it — including the footnote markers for organic-farming and essential-oil origin. The same list is published on the product page, so you can read it before you buy rather than after.

What do I do with the empty vessel?

Rinse it and recycle it where your local stream accepts it — the glass bottles and the jar are straightforward, and the cartons are card. The dispensing fittings (pump, dropper) are mixed-material parts and are usually not accepted kerbside; separate them first. An airless pump cannot be fully emptied, and that residue is unavoidable.

Should I patch test?

Yes — with any new formulation, ours included. Apply a little to the inner forearm and wait a day. Every product page lists declarable allergens under its full INCI.

Where are the formulations made?

Formulated and filled in small batches. Where an origin is settled we name it in the product's own description — Chilean rosehip, Moroccan argan and ghassoul, Ghanaian shea, Icelandic volcanic sand. Where a supply line is not yet fixed we would rather publish nothing than name a country we cannot stand behind; those origins go up as they are settled, and not before.
